    What 3.5 kg costs to keep flying

    In level flight lift equals weight, so speed fixes the lift coefficient. Drag and power follow from it.

    Level flight, trim drag excluded, constant propeller efficiency. A calculation from the study's parameters, not a flight test.

    Rolling a wing with no tail

    Differential elevon deflection rolls the aircraft. The study models bank angle as a first-order lag with a 0.25 s time constant.

    Elevon deflection is drawn in proportion to the command, for illustration. Turns use 25° and the limit is 35°.

    How the camera sets the flight lines

    Altitude fixes the ground footprint. Side overlap then fixes how far apart the strips fly.

    Forward overlap is 75 %. The manuscript's 58 % stereo-assisted claim is unverified.

    Where the follower finds free lift

    Each tip vortex lifts the air outside it. A follower flying there meets a small upwash and can trim to lower power.

    Upwash uses the study's Lamb-Oseen vortex model, followers 3 b behind. The power saving comes from the free-wake runs: 3.1 to 4.4 %, depending on an uncalibrated core radius. Swirl in the 3D view is drawn 6 times stronger for visibility.
